# Geometry gap analysis: reconstruction (forms v1) vs Astra R08

Compared panel by panel (front, three-quarter, side, head / shoulder-torso / arm / leg
detail) against `checkpoints/r08-forms-v1/` (the version before this pass).

## What R08 is actually built from

R08 is a sculpted, *knife-faceted* creature: every surface is broken into large irregular
planar facets with crisp chiselled edges (10–25 cm facets on the torso and limbs, smaller
around damage), over forms that are blocky in silhouette but never straight or uniform.
Damage is a **layered rust shell**: a 1.5–2.5 cm outer layer torn away in bark-like patches
with torn-paper sawtooth edges (the torn edge reads lighter tan), a darker red-brown
under-layer, and in the worst places a shallow cavity of dark, fibrous red tissue running
along the limb, with flat rusted bars driven in.

## Differences, most important first

| Area | Forms v1 (before) | R08 |
|---|---|---|
| Surface construction | Smooth lofts of chamfered boxes (12–16-sided sections, few rings): uniform planes, constant bevels, no facets | Sculpted surfaces with large irregular planar facets, crisp edges, dents and asymmetric bulges at several scales |
| Head | Symmetric stack of three barrel strata — reads as a clean drum; small sockets; no damage | Compact rounded block; irregular overhanging layers (crown cap, brow over the sockets, cheek band); deep square sockets; dents; big fracture on the upper left (monster's left) exposing red under-layers; torn mouth corner; chipped edges |
| Jaw | Thin smooth wedge, clean hinge knuckles | (Changed by design: forced/broken open.) Must stay heavy, irregular and damaged, with torn tissue at the hinge and corners |
| Torso | Clean rounded-rectangle loft | Same blocky silhouette but convex chest/belly planes, irregular bottom hem, faceted trapezius/top, rounded upper back; wounds at the right chest, lower belly (bent bracket), left shoulder junction (deep vertical wound), upper back (spike) |
| Shoulders | Rectangular arm-loft tops | Chiselled caps that slope outward, overlap the torso corners and break into crevices at the junction |
| Long arm | Straight box segments | Upper arm angled out with a slight bow, notch at the inner elbow, forearm thickening into a heavy blunt club, twisting cross-sections; heavy peeling around the elbow/forearm; forearm bar; dark end face |
| Short arm | Straight box | Slab with a slight outward bow, heavy peels on the lower half, bar at mid-forearm, blunt dark stump |
| Legs | Boxes on box feet | Blocky columns with knee swelling and uneven tapers; vertical fibrous knee wounds; chunky bevelled feet |
| Damage | None (forms stage) | Localised layered bark peels, shallow fibrous wounds, shell flaps lifting over wounds, bars with entry wounds |
| Materials | Uniform procedural rust | Granular stone-like rust with orange / brown / red-purple variation, dark speckle, light worn edges, light torn edges, red under-layer, dark crevices, old blood, rusted bars, stained teeth |

## Plan

Rebuild every body part as a dense sculpt (`scripts/sculpt.py`): nuanced spline lofts with
varying superellipse sections → voxel remesh → large-form brushes, trims and dents → knife
facets (decimate to large facets, jitter, dissolve coplanar triangles, re-densify with simple
subdivision so facets stay planar and edges crisp) → layered peels / shallow tissue / chips
driven by an R08 damage map → lifted shell plates and bars. Materials read the per-vertex
attributes the sculpt writes (layer, tissue, torn edge, convexity, cavity, blood, socket).

(A first attempt at faceting with Voronoi cell planes on the voxel grid produced curved,
serrated ridges and was replaced by the decimate-and-re-densify method.)

## Validation and refinement

Rendered front / three-quarter / exact side / head / mouth profile / shoulder-torso /
long-arm views with R08-matched cameras and the dark R08-style studio, compared side by side
(`model/renders_r08hd/_r08_vs_*.png`). Fixes made from those comparisons before presenting:

- the skull had grown during the rebuild (0.64 m wide, 0.40 m above the gum line, measured):
  resized back to R08 (0.55–0.58 m wide, 0.36 m), overall height 2.84 m;
- the strata read as continuous bands (a drum): replaced by one wavy, broken crown cap and a
  brow ledge on the face only, plus a rounder, asymmetric crown and swollen right cheek;
- facets too timid: fewer, larger facets with stronger angles;
- torn edges too busy and too pale: less high-frequency fringe, smaller lifted lips, thicker
  shell, browner under-layers, darker cavities; the colour palette darkened and given more
  mottling contrast;
- the heavier jaw cut into the chest when opened: jaw tapered toward the hinge, upper chest
  recessed (hidden by the jaw and the short arm), dislocation reduced; teeth, gums and tongue
  are clear of the body, only the jaw's rear is anchored in the hidden neck block;
- R08's long-arm club end is heavier, its shoulder caps chiselled: club flared, caps trimmed.

## Remaining differences (honest)

- **Surface crispness.** R08's facets are larger and sharper and its shell plates thicker with
  cleanly broken edges. Ours are planar with crisp facet edges, but the torn edges are
  finer-grained and busier, and some lifted rims stack into small blocky steps (most visible
  around the long-arm elbow).
- **Material.** R08 reads like a scanned rust texture with high-frequency, high-contrast
  mottling (red-purple to near black). Ours is procedural: tone and zoning are close, the
  surface is smoother and a little more orange in places.
- **Damage pattern.** Same placement as R08 (knees, elbows, forearms, torso sides, belly, upper
  back), with two strata, fibrous strands and lifted plates, but R08's knees and forearms read
  as shredded overlapping plates where ours read more as holes with lighter fringes.
- **Head.** Size, layout, sockets, ledges, fracture and bracket match; R08's crown is chunkier
  and rockier. The forced-open jaw is the intended change.
- **Teeth.** Crooked stained planks as in R08; R08's are slightly wider and flatter.
- **Budget.** 1.56 M triangles: design resolution, to be optimised and baked for Roblox after
  approval.
