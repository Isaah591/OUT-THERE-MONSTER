# OUT THERE — Monster Reconstruction (Blender)

A from-scratch Blender rebuild of the OUT THERE monster. The original Roblox screenshots
(`references/roblox_original_*`) define the anatomy and silhouette; the Astra R08 remaster
screenshots (`references/r08_remaster_surface_*`) are used **only** as the quality benchmark
for surface, wound and material treatment. All geometry is generated by Python, so every
proportion is a named, editable number and the model rebuilds in ~20 seconds.

## Rebuilding

Blender 5.0.1 is used as the `bpy` Python module (PyPI, Python 3.11) because the cloud
environment cannot reach download.blender.org. Cycles CPU works; EEVEE/Workbench do not
(no GPU / libEGL in the container). The scripts also run inside a normal Blender install.

```bash
python3.11 -m venv bvenv && bvenv/bin/pip install bpy pillow
```

```bash
bvenv/bin/python scripts/build_monster.py --stage final
```

```bash
bvenv/bin/python scripts/render_views.py model/out_there_monster.blend model/renders front threequarter side_r head_front mouth wound_leg
```

## How the geometry is constructed (v2)

- **Units / axes:** metres, Z up, monster faces −Y. Monster's right (−X) carries the long arm.
- **Body:** lofts of 12-point chamfered sections along spine/limb paths, with coherent
  noise bulges and dents, then low-frequency structural deformation. Torso, neck,
  deltoids, six vertebra knobs and two asymmetric scapula plates are boolean-unioned into
  one connected structure. Asymmetry is deliberate: dropped heavy right shoulder, collapsed
  left flank, withered short arm pinched at the elbow, twisting torso, tilted head.
- **Knapping:** broad, shallow planes slice corners and break straight edges (like chipped
  stone). Each cut has a jagged outline, so where the plane dips under the surface the
  step wall is a torn layer edge instead of a rectangle. A planar-dissolve pass then
  removes the coplanar slivers the booleans leave, at no change in shape.
- **Skull:** built from 13 superellipse rings with a per-vertex sculpt function — separate
  orbital hollows under heavy brow ridges, brows dragged down into a central furrow,
  cheekbone ledges, a caved-in dent on the upper left, a crushed rear corner, a slanted
  crown, a swollen right side. Then chisel, deformation and knapping. Sockets are jagged,
  tapered holes ray-cast onto the real surface, with fracture lines running out of them;
  the eyes are 1–1.2 cm red embers deep inside.
- **Jaw and hinge:** a heavy irregular jaw with ascending rami up to knuckle hinges. The
  monster's right hinge is split, its pin bent and sticking out — the reason that side sags
  7°. Jaw opening **55°**; the jaw's lower teeth, gums and tongue are checked to be clear of
  the chest on every build (BVH overlap test, currently 0). Flattened sinew bands run from
  the cheek ledges to the jaw; torn, wrinkled membranes stretch across the mouth corners.
- **Mouth interior:** palate ridges, a rotted tongue mass in the jaw trough, a ribbed throat
  receding into the body, and tissue strings stretched (some snapped) between palate and jaw.
- **Teeth:** 37 generated teeth in three size classes (giant slabs, normal, needles); kinked,
  twisted, snapped, chipped, stumps and split teeth; some rammed in at 50–80°, some pointing
  back into the throat, crowded second-row teeth, gaps, receded gums exposing dark roots.
  Smooth enamel shading with crisp fracture edges. Per-vertex attribute drives yellowing,
  decay pits, root grime and blood running down from the gums. Gums are swollen, lumpy and
  necrotic.
- **Wounds (shallow, layered):** wide ~1 cm *peels* strip the rust skin to an exposed, pitted
  steel under-layer; inside, *tears* split the shell (walls show its thickness). Each tear is
  filled with a corrugated fibrous tissue sheet whose ridges sit only 1–2 cm below the shell
  surface (projected onto the intact surface, edges tucked under the rim), with splinters
  hanging over the opening and, in two places, a rod driven into the wound.
- **Materials respond to geometry:** per-facet tint (`facet` attribute), ambient occlusion
  grime in crevices, worn edges from bevel-normal deviation, flaking-rust and dark
  oxidised-scale zones with their own roughness and relief, vertical grime streaks, dirt
  from the ground up, and a painted `blood` attribute (wounds, mouth drips, stump, rod
  entries) broken up into old, dark stains.

## Triangle budget (v2)

~59,000 triangles total. Per part: torso 16.8k, long arm 9.3k, skull 7.8k, jaw 6.6k,
legs 4.4k / 3.9k, short arm 4.0k, teeth 2.5k, gums 1.7k, tissue/interior/hinges ~1.7k.
Every part is under Roblox's 20,000-triangle MeshPart limit; the export groups in
`bake_export.py` (Body, Head, Jaw, limbs, Rods, Eyes) stay under it too.

## Known limitations

- No armature, weights or animation yet.
- Materials are procedural; Roblox only sees baked 1024² maps, which lose some fine detail.
  The v2 bake/export has not been run yet (deliberately — review first).
- Mouth-corner membranes and sinew bands are generated for the current jaw angle; changing
  `JAW_OPEN_DEG` needs a rebuild (or shape keys once rigged).
- Rods are one mesh spanning several body parts.
