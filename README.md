# OUT THERE — Monster Reconstruction (Blender)

A from-scratch Blender rebuild of the OUT THERE monster. **Current direction:** recreate the
Astra R08 design (`references/r08_reference_sheet_primary.webp`, detail panels in
`references/r08_remaster_surface_*.webp`) as faithfully as possible — proportions, silhouette,
sculpted faceted forms, layered rust damage, shallow wounds, bars and teeth — with exactly one
intentional change: the lower jaw is forced / broken open into a Pac-Man-like wedge.
All geometry is generated by Python (Blender as a module), so every form, wound and parameter
is editable and the model rebuilds in ~35 s.

## How it is built (R08 high-fidelity pass)

Units metres, Z up, monster faces −Y; its right (−X) carries the long arm.

Every body part is built the way a sculpt is, in order of scale (`sculpt.py`):

1. **Base form** — a spline loft through key rings whose sections are superellipses with their
   own exponent, twist and four independent half-extents (left/right, front/back), so cross-
   sections change shape along the part (e.g. the torso's convex belly, receding upper chest
   and flat back; the long arm's bowed upper arm, elbow and flaring club).
2. **Even surface** — voxel remesh (~1 cm) and smoothing.
3. **Large forms** — brushes: gaussian pushes (hunched upper back, belly, elbow knob, inner
   elbow notch, knee caps, cheekbones, heavy chin), planar trims (chiselled shoulder-cap tops
   and chamfers, chest plane), low-frequency warps for asymmetry, dents.
4. **Knife facets** — the low-poly sculpt step: decimate to a few hundred large facets, jitter
   the vertices so neighbouring planes meet at clear angles, dissolve near-coplanar triangles
   into irregular polygons, then re-densify with SIMPLE subdivision (×4), which keeps every
   facet planar and every edge straight and crisp.
5. **R08 damage map** — art-directed zones per part (elbows, forearms, knees, shins, torso
   sides, upper back, belly, the head's upper left...). Inside them the rust shell is torn
   away in two stepped strata (~2 cm shell, ~1.3 cm under-layer). The torn edges are snapped
   onto the field's iso-line, so they follow it instead of the voxel grid, with splinters
   running along the limb and a torn-paper sawtooth; plate edges above a hole lift and hang
   over it. In the wound zones a shallow cavity (2–5 cm) shows dark fibrous tissue with thick
   strands along the limb. Edges get occasional chips.
6. **Lifted shell plates** — thick jagged shell fragments hinged on torn rims and rotated
   outward over the wounds (rust on top, under-layer beneath, torn edges).
7. **Attributes** — `layer`, `tear`, `tissue`, `convex`, `cavity`, `blood`, `socket` are
   written per vertex; the materials read them, so colour, roughness and relief follow the
   geometry, and they can be baked for Roblox.

**Head (R08).** Compact, asymmetric rounded block (swollen right cheek, narrower rounded
crown, flatter face), a thick chipped crown cap and a brow ledge that overhangs the sockets
(broken, wavy, not continuous bands), knife facets, deep square sockets with chipped rims and
tiny red eyes, a vaulted palate, a large fracture on the upper left exposing the rust strata
and tissue with lifted plates, damage on the right side and back, a staple bracket in the crown.

**Jaw / mouth — the one intentional change.** The heavy U-shaped lower jaw (thickest at the
chin, tapering toward the rami, carved mouth floor, broken right side, chipped chin) is forced
open **66°** about its rear hinge and **dislocated**: the condyles have slid ~3 cm down out of
their sockets, so the drop is larger than a clean rotation. The upper skull, upper-jaw
projection and head size are unchanged, so the profile is a '<' / Pac-Man wedge. The hinge
sits where R08's jaw line meets the shoulder, so the whole wedge reads in the exact side view.
Empty sockets and bent hinge pins, torn cheek membranes bridging the rear of the wedge, taut
tendons (one snapped per side), swollen gums, crooked plank teeth on both jaws, palate ridges,
tongue and ribbed throat. Teeth, gums and tongue never touch the body; only the rear of the
jaw is anchored in the hidden neck block (checked on every build).

**Bars.** Rusted flat bars with rivets driven into entry wounds: back-of-shoulder spike, bent
belly hook, forearm, short-arm and shin stubs, crown staple bracket.

**Materials.** `M_R08_Rust`: granular, stone-like rust (multi-scale mottling, orange oxidation
and dull-brown zones, purple-brown blotches, dark pits, run-off streaks, dark grime patches),
lighter worn convex edges, darker grime in cavities and under overhangs (attribute + AO),
red-brown under-layers, a light tan torn fringe, dark wet fibrous tissue, old blood stains and
runs. `M_R08_Bar`: scaly dark rust with orange blooms and bare worn steel on the edges.

## Size

1.56 M triangles, 28.5 MB as a compressed .blend. Torso 364k, long arm 263k, jaw 198k,
legs 168k / 160k, short arm 168k, cranium 160k, bars 53k + crown bracket 17k; teeth, gums,
tissue, tongue, throat, hinges and eyes under 6k together (printed by `build_r08.py`). This is
a dense, sculpt-resolution design model, not a game mesh.

## Known limitations

- The facet layout, damage map and wound shapes are procedural and art-directed to R08, not
  copies of its hand sculpt; they match its language and placement, not stroke for stroke.
- Torn edges come from displacement on a ~6 mm grid plus separate lifted plates; true
  undercuts (shell overhanging a cavity) are limited to the plates.
- Materials are procedural; R08's surface reads as a scanned rust texture with finer variation.
- Not Roblox-ready yet: needs decimation/retopology to the 20k-triangle MeshPart limit, UVs and
  baking the attribute-driven materials (planned after approval).
- No armature, weights or animation.
